Why the gate matters

Same code ≠ same concept

The World Bank explicitly warns that identical historical series codes may have represented different base years or valuations across releases.

Fail closed

If metadata is insufficient or units changed, the correct product behavior is to refuse the comparison and show why.

Evidence promotion

Only validated rows move from imported data to comparable observations to a public evidence page with provenance and machine-readable exports.
