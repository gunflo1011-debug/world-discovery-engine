What “verified” means here

Official source

Published observations come from the World Bank's World Development Indicators and retain source and indicator identity.

Observation year stays visible

World Discovery does not silently mix years to fill missing values. The year shown with a value is the year of that observation.

Missing data stays missing

If an observation is unavailable for a selected year, the interface should show that gap rather than substitute a different year without saying so.

Limits to keep in mind

Coverage differs by indicator, country and year. A lower country count on one indicator does not mean the missing countries were excluded manually; it can simply reflect unavailable official observations for that period. See the methodology and source pages for more detail.
