How to read this indicator

DEFINITION

Used the internet in the last 3 months

The indicator counts individuals who used the Internet from any location during the previous three months, using devices such as computers, mobile phones or other connected equipment.

YEAR MATTERS

2024 is the observation year

The page was retrieved and assembled on 22 August 2026. That retrieval date does not make the observations “2026 data”. Every comparison above is explicitly a 2024 observation.

NO IMPUTATION

No mixed-year carry-forward

This view does not fill missing countries with older values. The first release uses a small verified same-year slice so ranking semantics remain defensible.
