How an indicator becomes REAL

1

Locate matching vintages

2

Validate comparability

Unit, series identity, entity type and known methodology context must support a defensible comparison.

3

Publish reproducible evidence

Only then do HTML evidence, machine-readable exports, indicator hubs and discovery summaries become public.
