Evidence types are explicit

1

Revision evidence

Archive-to-archive comparisons require vintage compatibility and publish exact historical provenance.

2

Current observation products

Same-year comparisons expose the observation year, retrieval date, source attribution and machine-readable records without claiming revision comparability.

3

Screening

Blocked candidates remain public only as transparent status pages where useful; unverified values do not enter discovery output.
