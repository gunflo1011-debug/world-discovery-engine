Economy · GDP per capita

GDP per capita (current US$)

Indicator NY.GDP.PCAP.CD. GDP per capita is gross domestic product divided by midyear population. This series is expressed in current U.S. dollars, so it is not an inflation-adjusted measure of living standards over time.

SOURCE VERIFIED · COUNTRY SNAPSHOT NOT YET PUBLISHED

Source and provenance

Publisher surface

World Bank World Development Indicators (WDI), Indicators API v2.

Underlying source organizations include country official statistics, national statistical organizations and/or central banks, OECD National Accounts data files, and World Bank staff estimates.

License

World Bank lists this indicator under CC BY 4.0. World Discovery will preserve attribution and retrieval metadata with the published snapshot.

Frequency and unit

Annual. Current U.S. dollars per person. Nominal, constant-price and PPP variants are distinct indicators and will not be mixed in one ranking.

Publication contract

The country comparison will publish only after an official WDI snapshot is ingested with country metadata, aggregates are excluded, numeric observations are validated, the observation year is explicit, and missing countries are not silently backfilled from another year. The page will then expose country values, comparison controls and machine-readable provenance.

Why no values yet? The source and license are verified, but this repository does not yet contain the validated GDP-per-capita country snapshot. Publishing copied or inferred numbers would violate the site's fail-closed methodology.
