What makes these pages evidence?

Two archived vintages

The comparison uses official WDI archive releases dated 28 January and 2 July 2025 rather than silently mixing current values.

Fail-closed comparability

Country, indicator, unit and reference year must match. Regional and income aggregates are excluded from this evidence family.

Reusable by people and machines

Each evidence route provides readable HTML plus JSON and CSV exports with exact source URLs and publication vintages.
