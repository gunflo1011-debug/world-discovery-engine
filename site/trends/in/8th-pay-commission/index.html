8th Pay Commission: what is confirmed today?

India's 8th Central Pay Commission is on the second and final scheduled day of its Chennai stakeholder consultations on 8 September 2026. No official fitment factor, final salary hike, pension increase or implementation payout date has been announced.

Last updated: 8 September 2026

Why is the 8th Pay Commission trending now?

Google Trends shows 8th pay commission as an active India trend in the past 24 hours, with a 5K+ search-volume bucket and roughly 200% growth. The Commission's official calendar confirms that 8 September is the second day of its Chennai visit, where employee and pensioner organisations are presenting views on pay, allowances, pensions and service conditions.

Has the fitment factor been fixed?

No. Figures circulating in news reports and employee-body proposals are demands or scenarios, not an official government-approved fitment factor. The Commission is still gathering evidence and stakeholder submissions before making recommendations.

What is the official timeline?

The Government of India constituted the 8th Central Pay Commission on 3 November 2025 and gave it 18 months to submit its recommendations. The Cabinet's Terms of Reference say the Commission may also issue interim reports if it considers them necessary.

When could revised pay take effect?

The government's Cabinet note says that, based on the usual ten-year Pay Commission cycle, the effect of the recommendations would normally be expected from 1 January 2026. That wording does not by itself confirm when revised salaries or arrears will actually be paid; implementation requires recommendations and subsequent government decisions.

What happens after Chennai?

The Commission's published schedule lists a Puducherry consultation for 9 September, followed by Chandigarh on 16–18 September 2026 and Bengaluru on 7–8 October. The milestones that matter most are any formal Commission recommendation or interim report and the government's later implementation decision.
