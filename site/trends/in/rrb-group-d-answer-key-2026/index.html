RRB Group D answer key 2026: is it out?

Yes. Railway Recruitment Boards released the provisional CEN 09/2025 Level-1 question papers, recorded responses and answer keys on 7 September 2026. Candidates should use their regional RRB website and candidate login to view their own response sheet and submit any objection within the official window.

Last updated: 8 September 2026

What was released?

The release covers the Level-1 CBT under CEN 09/2025: candidates can view the questions they received, their recorded responses and the provisional answer keys. The CBT was held in August 2026.

How do I check my RRB Group D response sheet?

Open the official website of the RRB region you applied through, find the CEN 09/2025 Level-1 answer-key or response-sheet notice, and use the candidate login. Do not rely on third-party pages asking for credentials.

Can I challenge an answer?

Yes. RRBs provide an online objection facility for disputed questions, options or provisional keys. Current reports citing the official notice say the viewing and objection facility opened on 7 September at 17:00 IST and is available until 15 September at 17:00 IST. Because RRB portals can update notices, candidates should confirm the closing time shown in their own regional RRB login before paying an objection fee.

How much does an objection cost?

Current reports citing the RRB notice state a fee of ₹50 per question plus applicable bank charges. If an objection is accepted, the fee for that valid objection is refundable after applicable bank charges.

Is this the final answer key or the result?

No. This is the provisional answer-key stage. RRBs review valid objections before finalising answers and later publishing recruitment results. A provisional score estimate is not the same as the official result.
