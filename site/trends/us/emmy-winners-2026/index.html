Emmy winners 2026: who has won so far?

The 2026 Emmys are not finished yet. The Television Academy has announced winners from the first two Creative Arts nights on September 5 and 6. The main 78th Emmy Awards ceremony is scheduled for September 14, 2026, so the final major-category winners are still pending.

Last updated: 8 September 2026.

Top winners from the Creative Arts nights

Widow’s Bay led night two with eight Emmys. DTF St. Louis won six, while Spider-Noir won five and The Pitt and Pluribus won four each. On night one, the Apple Music Super Bowl LX Halftime Show Starring Bad Bunny led with seven wins.

Have the main Emmy winners been announced?

No. The televised 78th Emmy Awards ceremony is scheduled for September 14 at 8 p.m. ET / 5 p.m. PT on NBC and Peacock. Searches for “Emmy winners 2026” before then are mainly referring to the Creative Arts results already announced.

Where can I see the official full list?

The Television Academy maintains the official 2026 nominees-and-winners database and separate official recaps for nights one and two.
