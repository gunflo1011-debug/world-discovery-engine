Labor Day 2026: what is open and closed today?

Labor Day is Monday, September 7, 2026. U.S. stock markets, most bank branches, federal offices and post offices are closed today. Many major retailers and grocery stores are open, but individual store hours can vary.

Quick answers

Is the stock market open on Labor Day 2026?

No. The New York Stock Exchange and Nasdaq are closed on Monday, September 7, 2026. Regular U.S. stock trading resumes Tuesday, September 8.

Are banks open on Labor Day?

Most bank branches are closed. Labor Day is a federal holiday and major banks generally close their branches. ATMs, websites and mobile-banking apps remain available, although some transactions may not process until the next business day.

Is USPS delivering mail on Labor Day?

No. USPS says all Post Office locations are closed on Monday, September 7, 2026, with regular mail delivery and retail services resuming Tuesday, September 8. Online services and many self-service kiosks remain available.

Are stores open on Labor Day?

Many retailers are open. Labor Day is a major shopping day, but opening hours vary by chain and location, so shoppers should check their local store before travelling.

What is Labor Day?

Labor Day is a U.S. federal holiday observed on the first Monday in September to honor the social and economic contributions of American workers.

Why is Labor Day celebrated?

The holiday grew out of the labor movement in the late nineteenth century. According to the U.S. Department of Labor, labor activists pushed for a national day recognizing workers' contributions to the country's strength, prosperity and well-being.

When did Labor Day become a federal holiday?

The first Labor Day celebration was held in New York City on September 5, 1882. States began adopting the holiday in the following years, and Congress made the first Monday in September a legal holiday in 1894.
