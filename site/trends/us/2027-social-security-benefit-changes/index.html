2027 Social Security benefit changes: what is actually confirmed?

As of September 8, 2026, the Social Security Administration has not announced the 2027 cost-of-living adjustment (COLA). SSA says the next COLA will be announced in October 2026. Under current law, the full retirement age is already 67 for people born in 1960 or later; there is no automatic new increase to age 68 or 69 starting in 2027.

Last updated: 8 September 2026.

What is confirmed for 2027 right now?

The most important confirmed rule is the existing full-retirement-age schedule. SSA states that people born in 1960 or later have a full retirement age of 67. People can still claim retirement benefits as early as 62, but claiming before full retirement age permanently reduces the monthly retirement benefit.

Has the 2027 Social Security COLA been announced?

No. SSA's official COLA page currently lists the 2026 COLA at 2.8% and states that the next COLA will be announced in October 2026. Any specific 2027 COLA percentage circulating before that announcement is therefore an estimate, not an official SSA figure.

Is the retirement age increasing again in 2027?

Not under current law. The scheduled increase enacted decades ago has already reached 67 for people born in 1960 or later. Some SSA actuarial pages discuss proposals that would raise the retirement age beginning with people reaching age 62 in 2027, but those pages explicitly describe proposed provisions used to model Social Security solvency. They are not the current-law retirement schedule.

What other 2027 numbers are still pending?

Several annual Social Security figures are normally updated using inflation or wage data, including the taxable maximum and earnings-test limits. The official SSA COLA information available on September 8 still publishes the 2026 figures, so World Discovery will not present projected 2027 thresholds as final.

What should beneficiaries watch next?

The next major official update is SSA's October 2026 COLA announcement. After that, SSA typically publishes the associated annual benefit and program thresholds. Your personal benefit amount can differ from headline averages because it depends on your earnings record, claiming age, deductions and other individual factors.

FAQ

Will everyone get a Social Security increase in January 2027?

The annual COLA, if positive, generally affects Social Security and SSI payments, but the exact 2027 percentage has not yet been announced.

Did Social Security raise full retirement age to 68 in 2027?

No current SSA retirement-age table says that. Current law reaches 67 for people born in 1960 or later. Proposals to raise the age further should not be confused with enacted law.

When will the official 2027 COLA be known?

SSA says it will announce the next COLA in October 2026.
