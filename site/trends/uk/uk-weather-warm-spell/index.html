UK weather warm spell: is a heatwave coming?

Not immediately. The Met Office forecast for Tuesday 8 September says temperatures are around or slightly below average across much of the UK on Wednesday, while the 12–21 September outlook is expected to be close to average overall. Some rather warm days are possible later in the period, especially in southern areas, but the current official outlook does not describe an imminent nationwide heatwave.

Last updated: 8 September 2026.

What the Met Office currently says

The national forecast says Wednesday should be largely dry with sunny spells, with temperatures around or slightly below the seasonal average. The longer-range forecast for 12–21 September remains changeable, with rain or showers more likely in northern and western areas and more settled periods possible in the south. Temperatures are expected to average close to normal, although some warmer days are possible.

So why is “UK weather warm spell” trending?

Recent headlines have discussed the possibility of warmer September weather, but longer-range forecasts are probabilistic and can change. The safest reading of the current official forecast is that brief warmer conditions are possible later in September, rather than that a confirmed heatwave is about to begin.

Could this forecast change?

Yes. The Met Office updates its long-range outlook daily and stresses that confidence is lower further into the future. Check the latest local forecast and any official weather warnings before making weather-sensitive plans.
