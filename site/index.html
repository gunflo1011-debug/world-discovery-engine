What makes this different?

1

Historical vintages

We preserve what a dataset said at different publication dates instead of overwriting history.

2

Fail-closed comparisons

If units or methodology are not comparable, the engine refuses to manufacture a revision.

3

Citation-ready output

Every public fact is designed to have a stable page, raw values, provenance, method and machine-readable exports.
