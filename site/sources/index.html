Sources, provenance & machine access

Every preferred discovery must trace to an official or explicitly licensed dataset and a reproducible transformation. REAL revision evidence and CURRENT_VERIFIED indicator observations are exposed in human-readable and machine-readable form.

Current production status: three verified product families are published: REAL population-revision evidence, CURRENT_VERIFIED Internet Use observations for IT.NET.USER.ZS, and CURRENT_VERIFIED GDP per capita observations for NY.GDP.PCAP.CD. Internet Use and GDP per capita use official same-year snapshots and are not historical-revision products. Real-GDP revision publishing remains blocked where release-specific methodology comparability is not established.

Why archive comparison is fail-closed

Matching series codes alone do not prove that two historical releases are methodologically comparable. Base years, valuation conventions, definitions and other metadata can change across releases. A revision is published only when the specific pair passes comparability checks.

GateRequired before a revision is published
Dataset identitySame official source family and known release/vintage
Series semanticsIndicator definition, unit and relevant valuation/base-year semantics compatible
GeographySame country/entity identity, not merely a matching display label
Observation periodSame year/period and frequency
ProvenanceSource URL, vintage date and import manifest retained
TransformationCalculation reproducible from preserved raw/normalized values

Evidence promotion ladder

1

Imported

A release or current observation is ingested with a stable official source reference and retained provenance.

2

Validated

Revision products pass pair-specific comparability checks; current-indicator products pass same-year, entity, unit and source checks.

3

Published

The discovery receives an appropriate stable human surface plus machine-readable data and discovery metadata. Anything incomplete remains blocked from preferred discovery.
