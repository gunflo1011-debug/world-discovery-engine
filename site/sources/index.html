Provenance before publication

Sources & data provenance

Every published discovery must trace back to an official or explicitly licensed dataset, a known release/vintage and a reproducible transformation.

Current MVP status: visible evidence examples are still labelled DEMO. They are not presented as real historical revisions. Real evidence is only promoted after two compatible source vintages pass provenance and comparability checks.

Primary source: World Development Indicators

The World Bank's World Development Indicators (WDI) is the first production source for the engine.

OFFICIAL

Current WDI dataset

The World Bank Data Catalog describes WDI as a public dataset with annual country-level indicators. The catalog currently lists coverage from 1960–2025 and a July 2026 update.

Open World Bank catalog →
ARCHIVE

WDI Database Archives

World Bank DataBank exposes previous WDI versions going back to electronic releases from 1989. This is the key source for reconstructing what the database said at different publication dates.

Open WDI archives →
LICENSE

Reuse & attribution

The World Bank catalog marks WDI as Public and licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. World Discovery Engine keeps source attribution on evidence pages and machine-readable exports.

View licensing metadata →

Why archive comparison is fail-closed

The World Bank explicitly warns that identical WDI series codes may have represented different base years or valuations in older releases. For example, constant-dollar GDP series have changed base years across editions. Therefore, the engine does not treat matching indicator codes alone as proof of comparability.
