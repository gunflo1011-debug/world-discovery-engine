Source and interpretation

The economy catalog is sourced from the World Bank World Development Indicators. Coverage and latest available year vary by indicator and geography, so the year shown beside each observation matters. Current-US-dollar measures are nominal and should not be read as inflation-adjusted changes in living standards.
